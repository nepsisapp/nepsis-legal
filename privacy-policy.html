Privacy Policy  ·  Effective Date: August 19, 2026

Welcome to Nepsis. Your privacy matters deeply to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we retain it, and how we protect it when you use the Nepsis mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to provide and maintain the Nepsis experience.

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a unique account identifier. Authentication credentials are processed securely through Firebase Authentication. Nepsis does not have access to your unencrypted password.
  • Usage and technical information: We may collect limited information about how the app is accessed and used, such as features opened, app version, device platform, technical errors, and general performance information. This information may be used to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Nepsis.
  • Purchase information: If you purchase access to Nepsis, payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We may receive limited transaction and entitlement information necessary to confirm your purchase and provide access. We do not collect or store your complete payment-card or banking information.
  • Communications: If you contact us for support, we may collect your email address, the contents of your message, and any information you voluntarily provide so that we can respond.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not collect health, biometric, or medical information.
  • We do not record or listen to you through your microphone.
  • We do not access your camera, contacts, or personal media.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your personal information for targeted advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information described above to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage your Nepsis account.
  • Verify your purchase and provide access to app content.
  • Restore previously purchased access.
  • Deliver streamed images, videos, and written experiences.
  • Respond to support requests and communications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the app.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on where you live, we may process your personal information because it is necessary to provide the service you requested, because we have a legitimate interest in operating and securing Nepsis, because you have provided consent, or because processing is necessary to comply with applicable law.

5. Data Storage and Security

Account and app information may be stored and processed using Google Firebase and other service providers that support the operation of Nepsis.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information, including your email address and authentication identifier, for as long as your Nepsis account remains active or as necessary to provide the app's services.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by using the Delete Account option available within the app or by contacting us at hellonepsis@gmail.com .

When an account-deletion request is received, the account and associated personal data will generally be deleted within 30 days, unless retention is necessary for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or the protection of legal rights.

Residual copies may remain in secure system backups for up to 90 days before being removed through normal backup-deletion processes. Information retained in backups is not actively used during that period.

Anonymous or aggregated information that can no longer reasonably identify you may be retained for security, analytics, troubleshooting, and service-improvement purposes.

Deleting your Nepsis account does not automatically delete transaction records maintained independently by Apple, Google, or other payment providers.

7. Third-Party Services

Nepsis relies on third-party services to provide parts of the app. These providers may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, cloud database, file storage, and technical infrastructure.
  • Apple App Store: App distribution, purchase processing, and purchase records for Apple users.
  • Google Play: App distribution, purchase processing, and purchase records for Android users.
  • RevenueCat: Purchase verification, entitlement status, and purchase restoration.

Nepsis does not control the independent privacy practices of these services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies for more information.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited information only:

  • With service providers that help us operate, secure, and deliver Nepsis.
  • When necessary to verify purchases or restore access.
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid government request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of Nepsis, its users, or others.
  • In connection with a business transfer, such as a merger, acquisition, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate privacy protections.

9. International Data Processing

Nepsis and its service providers may process or store information in the United States or other countries. These locations may have data protection laws that differ from those in your place of residence. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international data transfers.

10. Children's Privacy

Nepsis is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child under 13 has provided personal information to Nepsis, please contact us. We will take reasonable steps to verify the request and remove the information where required.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request a copy of certain personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Submit a complaint to an applicable data protection authority.

To exercise an available privacy right, use the Delete Account option in the app when applicable or contact us at hellonepsis@gmail.com .

We may need to verify your identity before completing certain privacy requests. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right available under applicable law.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Nepsis features, technologies, service providers, or legal obligations change. When changes are made, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.

Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or request consent before material changes take effect.
