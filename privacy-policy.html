Privacy Policy  ·  Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Welcome to Nepsis. Your privacy matters deeply to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we retain it, and how we protect it when you use the Nepsis mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide you with the Nepsis experience:

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address. Authentication credentials are processed securely through Firebase Authentication. Nepsis does not have access to your unencrypted password.
  • Usage data: We may collect limited information about how you use the app, such as which features are accessed, to maintain and improve the experience.
  • Purchase information: If you purchase access to Nepsis, payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store your full payment-card or banking information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not collect health or biometric data.
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use your personal information for advertising purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create, authenticate, and manage your Nepsis account.
  • Provide access to content based on your purchase status.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the app experience.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized access.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored using Google Firebase, which provides industry-standard security controls. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account information, including your email address and authentication identifier, for as long as your Nepsis account remains active or as necessary to provide the app's services.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by using the Delete Account option available in the app or by contacting us at hellonepsis@gmail.com.

When an account-deletion request is received, the account and associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days, unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory purposes.

Residual copies may remain in secure system backups for up to 90 days before being permanently removed. Information retained in backups is not used for any other purpose during that period.

Anonymous or aggregated information that can no longer reasonably identify an individual may be retained for analytics, security, and service-improvement purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Nepsis uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — authentication, database, and data storage
  • Google Play and Apple App Store — purchases and payment processing
  • RevenueCat — purchase status and entitlement management

7. Children's Privacy

Nepsis is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To delete your account, use the Delete Account option in the app or contact us at hellonepsis@gmail.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When changes are made, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy from time to time.
